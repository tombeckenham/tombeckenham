§ I   |   Now  ·  The Workshop p. 04 — Building OpenStory
I.

Script to screen, consistent
every shot.

A scene-by-scene pipeline that holds character, location, and visual style across an entire film — open source, MIT.

StackTypeScript · Bun · TanStack · Cloudflare Workers · React
FocusAI video · agentic systems · open source
LicenseMIT · openstory.so
Persistence is the unsolved problem of AI filmmaking.

Paste a script and OpenStory returns a scene-by-scene breakdown, a set of generated frames, and motion video for each shot. Characters, locations and the visual grammar of the film persist across every frame — the unsolved problem of AI filmmaking, addressed not by another model, but by a runtime that orchestrates them.

The platform is built in the open under the MIT license at openstory-so/openstory, and runs on TanStack Start with Cloudflare Workers as the execution layer. It is in active development.
